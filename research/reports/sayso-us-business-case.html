America pays SaySo more, and sues faster

7 Law

Wiretap law, not price, decides where to launch

Federal law needs only one person's consent, and the salesperson is a party. But about 11–13 states require everyone recorded to consent, including the customer.

9 Next steps for the US

Build the legal design before the first US pilot

  1. Make staff-only mode real in engineering: customer audio never leaves the badge, no customer voice templates, deletion logged.
  2. Make it real in contracts: no training on retailer data, "service provider" terms, written staff consent and voice-enrolment release.
  3. Start SOC 2 Type I now. The 6-month observation window for Type II is the longest wait on the list.
  4. Assemble badges in India to avoid the China-specific tariffs, then get FCC testing on the pre-certified module.
  5. Pilot in Texas or Georgia with a jewellery or mattress operator. Measure speech-hours, opt-out rate, conversion lift, and how many customers mind the badge light.
