# SaySo Competitors and Pricing (in-store conversation intelligence, phone CI benchmarks, retail video analytics)

Research date: 28 Oct 2026. Labels: **[Official]** = vendor's own page; **[3P est.]** = third-party, often a competitor's page or buyer reports; **[Vendor claim]** = self-reported, not checked.

## Q1. Which companies record and AI-analyse in-store / face-to-face sales conversations?

### Takeaway
"In-person conversation intelligence" is now a funded category. The US leaders are **Rilla** (about $757M valuation) and **Siro** ($50M Series B). Both are app- or device-based and sell mainly to field sales, home services and auto dealerships. Retail-floor specialists are smaller and newer: **Vera AI** (LatAm/Europe brands), **tolqAI** (GCC/Arabic) and **Borentis** (India, Gurgaon, Hinglish). None of them pairs audio with CCTV video.

### Cited Findings
**Rilla (formerly Rillavoice), New York, US**
- Records and transcribes in-person sales conversations, including on the showroom floor and in the service drive. — [Rilla](https://www.rilla.com/); [Borentis comparison](https://borentis.com/learn/best-ai-in-store-sales-coaching-tools-india/)
- Hardware: originally a phone app or device carried by the rep (product described as "virtual ridealongs"). — [Komo](https://komo.ai/directory/rilla)
- Funding: $3.7M seed (Crew Capital), 2022. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/01/uipath-speech-analytics-sales-teams-software-rillavoice/)
- Series B June 2025 led by GV: $68.5M primary capital, about $757M post-money, about $78.9M total raised. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/rilla)
- Valuation estimates disagree: StartupHub's model gives about $312M. — [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/rilla)
- Revenue: about $40M ARR in 2024 — [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/rilla). Sacra estimates about $51M (2025) and about $70M (Apr 2026). — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/rilla) [3P est.]
- Customers named: Benjamin Franklin Plumbing, One Hour Heating & Air, Mister Sparky Electric (home services). — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/c/Rilla)
- ROI: 40% close-rate lift and 17% higher ticket size — [Komo](https://komo.ai/directory/rilla); also cited as "$53 average ticket lift" — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/c/Rilla). [Vendor claim]

**Siro, US**
- AI sales coaching for in-person teams. For dealerships it records on the sales floor, in F&I and at the service drive, and ties recordings to the deal in the DMS. — [Siro dealerships](https://www.siro.ai/industry/dealerships)
- Hardware: a mobile app records the conversations. — [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/the-wire/siro-raises-50m-series-b-to-bring-ai-to-in-person-sales/)
- Funding: $50M Series B, May 2025, led by SignalFire, with 01 Advisors, StepStone, CRV, Fika and Index. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/siro-50-million-series-b-raised-for-bringing-ai-to-in-person-sales/). Seedtable lists about $75M total (unverified). — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/siro)

**Vera AI (heyvera.ai)**: in-store conversation intelligence
- A dedicated device for retail associates records floor conversations. It can also run on a PC, a phone or the customer's own hardware, and needs no cameras or POS integration. — [Vera AI](https://www.heyvera.ai/)
- Scores each conversation on 50+ criteria (discovery, objection handling, closing, product knowledge). Gives associates feedback, gives HQ insight on customer needs, and offers AI roleplay for new hires. Works "in any language". — [Vera AI](https://www.heyvera.ai/)
- Brand logos shown: Boggi, Roberts (fashion), Nissan, Hyundai, GAC (auto), Steren, Tigo (electronics/telecom), Farma24 (pharmacy). Site is in English and Spanish. — [Vera AI](https://www.heyvera.ai/)
- Positioning stat: mystery shopping covers "at best, 0.2%" of interactions. — [Vera AI](https://www.heyvera.ai/) [Vendor claim]
- Pricing, funding and HQ are not disclosed.

**tolqAI**: in-person conversation intelligence, GCC focus
- Defines the category as capturing face-to-face conversations in stores, dealerships, showrooms, hotels and clinics with "dedicated in-store hardware worn or placed by staff". Targets retail chains, luxury, auto, jewellery/watches, electronics, furniture, hospitality and healthcare. — [tolqAI blog](https://tolqai.com/blog/in-person-conversation-intelligence)
- Supports Arabic dialects and English–Arabic code-mixing. — [tolqAI pricing](https://tolqai.com/pricing)

**Borentis (Gurgaon, India)**: the closest India competitor to SaySo's audio half
- Offline-first capture of in-store sales conversations in Hinglish, Hindi and regional languages ("50+ Indian languages" per one description). Scores advisors on a 0–100 "Borentis Score" across six dimensions (objection handling, competitor defence, and others). Shares top performers' conversations. Sends automated WhatsApp/SMS/voice follow-up with revenue attribution. Described as "audio-first (video-ready)". — [Borentis](https://borentis.com/learn/best-ai-in-store-sales-coaching-tools-india/)
- Target sectors: consumer electronics, jewellery, automobile, real estate, BFSI. Address: Pioneer Urban Square, Sector 62, Gurgaon. No customers, funding or pricing disclosed. The comparison page is Borentis's own (Aug 2026). — [Borentis](https://borentis.com/learn/best-ai-in-store-sales-coaching-tools-india/)

**DealerPulse**: dealership app that records in-person sales conversations and runs AI analysis. — [aitools.inc](https://aitools.inc/tools/dealerpulse-ai)

**Seeed Studio "Smart Retail Voice AI"**: edge hardware with mic arrays and noise suppression that captures and transcribes customer–staff talk on-device over background music. It is a reference solution or OEM building block, not a SaaS competitor, and is relevant as a hardware supplier option. — [Seeed wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/solutions/smart-retail-voice-ai-solution-1/)

**Adjacent: retail voice wearables (communication, not analytics)**
- **Theatro**: key-fob-sized (about 1.5 oz) voice wearable with earpiece for store associates. Sold as SaaS per employee per month with the hardware bundled ("Software + Mobile Hardware as a Service"). Deployed with Walgreens; Honeywell resells it. — [Coresight](https://coresight.com/?p=10092); [Retail IT Insights](https://www.retailitinsights.com/doc/tech-start-up-employee-mobility-offering-markets-wearable-0001)
- Walmart "Ask Sam" and Target "Store Companion" are internal associate assistants, not conversation recorders. — [Retail Tech Innovation Hub](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/how-ai-wearables-are-transforming-retail-operations-customer-experience-and-store-productivity)

### Inferences
- The US/global category is proven with real money: Rilla's roughly $50–70M ARR in home services shows that "record every in-person pitch and coach" sells. Retail-floor-specific players (Vera, tolq, Borentis) are early, sub-scale and price per user.
- Borentis is the direct Indian threat: same thesis, Hinglish, offline-first, and jewellery/auto/electronics verticals. SaySo should get a demo or mystery-buy it.
- Theatro's per-employee bundled-hardware SaaS model is a useful precedent for pricing a SaySo badge.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Vera AI, Borentis, DealerPulse or Theatro.
- Could not confirm which hardware Rilla uses today (app vs dedicated device).
- No funding info found for Vera, tolq or Borentis.
- Did not find other Indian players (e.g., in-house tools at Titan, Tanishq, Maruti dealers); possible that some exist unpublicised.

## Q2. What do in-person CI tools charge?

### Takeaway
Typical prices are $79–$350 per rep per month, usually on annual contracts with a 5-seat minimum and a $1.5–5K setup fee. US prices are far above what Indian retail will pay per floor salesperson. The one official figure is tolqAI at $79/user/month.

### Cited Findings
- **tolqAI**: Standard from **$79/user/month** (about 296 SAR), covering scorecards, dashboards and Arabic dialect support. Enterprise is custom. "Nothing extra": onboarding, scorecards and pilot setup are included, and no hardware cost is listed. — [tolqAI pricing](https://tolqai.com/pricing) **[Official]**
- **Rilla**: $199–$349/rep/month, annual contract, 5-seat minimum, $1,500–5,000 setup — [SalesAsk](https://www.salesask.com/alternatives/rilla/rilla-pricing-guide-2026). Also cited at $300–500/user/month — [Main Street Picks](https://mainstreetpicks.com/hvac/rep-coaching-software/rilla) — and $4,000–5,000/seat/year per Sacra, cited by [Roonly](https://roonly.ai/best/ai-coaching-apps). **[3P est.; SalesAsk is a competitor]**
- **Siro**: $149–$249/rep/month, annual upfront, 5-seat minimum, $1,500–5,000 setup — [SalesAsk Siro guide](https://www.salesask.com/blog-posts/siro-pricing-guide-2026-costs-plans-better-alternatives). About $250/rep/month plus implementation — [Roonly](https://roonly.ai/best/ai-coaching-apps). **[3P est.]**
- SalesAsk positions itself as a cheaper alternative "from $150/month". — [SalesAsk](https://www.salesask.com/blog-posts/rilla-pricing-alternatives-comparison-2026) [Vendor claim]
- Adjacent dealership AI (chat/voice, not in-person recording): DealerAI $599–$2,299 per rooftop/month — [DealerAI pricing](https://dealerai.com/pricing) **[Official]**. TECOBI $1,995–$3,995/month plus setup (via a 2026 roundup, [CARVID](https://www.carvidapp.com/blog/best-ai-chatbots-for-car-dealerships/)) **[3P]**.

### Inferences
- At ₹83/USD, $79/user/month is about ₹6,500 and $200 is about ₹16,600 per salesperson per month. That is plausible for Indian luxury, jewellery or auto showrooms, but likely too high for mass apparel or electronics floor staff earning ₹15–25K/month. SaySo probably needs per-store pricing or a sub-₹2,000/badge/month tier for mass retail.

### Gaps
- No official Rilla or Siro price exists; all figures come from buyer reports or competitors.

## Q3. Phone-call conversation intelligence pricing benchmarks (incl. India)

### Takeaway
The global enterprise benchmark is Gong at about $1,300–1,920/user/year plus a platform fee. Contact-centre CI is about $40–70/agent/month. Indian vendors (Convin, Salesken, Enthu) are mostly quote-based, with reported entry points around $40–100/user/month.

### Cited Findings
- **Gong**: Foundations about $1,300–1,920/user/year. Platform fee $5–10K/yr (<50 users), $15–30K (50–250), $30–50K+ (enterprise). Onboarding $7.5K–65K. Bundles about $2,880–3,000/user/yr. 2–3 year contracts with 5–15% renewal uplift. A usage-based AI credit meter was reportedly added in 2026. — [Sybill](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/gong-pricing); [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/gong-pricing); [tl;dv](https://tldv.io/blog/gong-pricing/) **[3P est.]**
- **Observe.AI** (contact centre): quote-based. An AWS Marketplace listing gives Real-Time AI at **$828/agent/year (about $69/agent/month)** on a 12-month contract. — [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/observe-ai-complete-guide); [MavenAGI](https://www.mavenagi.com/blog/observe-ai-pricing) **[3P citing marketplace listing]**
- **Convin** (India, Bengaluru): Premium **$41.67/user/month** on the HubSpot marketplace — [HubSpot](https://ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/apps/convin-353510). $50/user/month — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/convin-ai/pricing). Spendbase lists a $50 Professional tier and a free plan — [Spendbase](https://www.spendbase.co/nl/?p=36535). An "INR 80/month" listing exists but is unverified — [Krowdbase](https://www.krowdbase.com/software/convin). G2 says to contact the vendor. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/convin-ai/pricing) **[Mixed: marketplace listing is closest to official]**
- **Salesken** (India): Capterra gives "Starting $99, flat rate, free trial" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.in/software/210355/salesken). SpotSaaS says custom/quote-based — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/salesken/pricing). Model: per-recorded-user licence plus platform fee — [Amotions (competitor)](https://amotionsinc.com/compare/salesken-alternative). **[3P, conflicting]**
- **Enthu.AI** (India/US): the official page has no rates. It is custom, in USD, billed semi-annually or annually, with a 14-day trial and 30-day PoC — [Enthu.AI pricing](https://enthu.ai/pricing/) **[Official]**. $45/user/month reported — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/compare/enthu-ai-vs-convin) **[3P, unverified]**.
- **Squadstack, GreyLabs, Gnani.ai** (India) offer conversation analytics or QA. No pricing found. — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/top-voice-ai-solutions-india-2026)

### Inferences
- Indian phone-CI pricing (about $40–50/seat, or ₹3,500–4,200) is a realistic anchor for what Indian buyers already pay for audio QA per seat. In-store audio is harder (noise, code-switching, offline), which may justify a premium, but buyers will compare against this.

### Gaps
- No official INR price list for Convin, Salesken or Squadstack.

## Q4. Retail video analytics / footfall / CCTV AI players and pricing (global + India)

### Takeaway
Footfall counting is commoditised: $9–150 per store per month plus $300–1,150 sensors (official US prices). Full CCTV-AI analytics in India runs about ₹800–1,200 per camera per month, or ₹8,000–14,000 per store per month (vendor-published). Wobot is the best-known Indian retail and food-service CCTV-AI startup but is small (about $2.5–5.5M raised).

### Cited Findings
**Global footfall / people counting** (table compiled by SMS Storetraffic, a competitor, from vendor pages)
- **Dor**: $300 sensor, $150/location/month ($135 billed annually), no install. **[Official per Dor's public page]**
- **SMS Storetraffic PEARL**: $499.95 sensor, software $0–51.95/store/month. **[Official]**
- **V-Count**: $299–799 sensor, $9–49/month. **[Official ranges]**
- **FootfallCam**: about $650–750 sensor plus a mandatory, unpriced support contract.
- **RetailNext**: about $146/store/month for 1 store and 1 sensor (calculator estimate, install extra). — all from [StoreTraffic comparison](https://storetraffic.com/best-people-counters/)
- RetailNext enterprise deals are said to typically land at "$5,000+ per door, multi-year" [3P est. by competitor Dor] — [Dor vs RetailNext](https://www.getdor.com/compare/dor-vs-retailnext). Enterprise vendors (Sensormatic/ShopperTrak, RetailNext) quote "from around $1,000/month" per store [3P rule of thumb] — [GrowthFactor](https://www.growthfactor.ai/resources/blog/retail-traffic-software-ultimate-guide)
- **Density** prices per space and targets offices and corporate real estate, not retail. — [Dor comparison](https://www.getdor.com/compare/best-people-counters)
- Integrator benchmark for AI loss-prevention camera systems: $8K–35K per small store, $15K–60K per store for regional rollouts. — [Tec-Tel](https://tec-tel.com/data/retail-loss-prevention-ai-pricing) **[3P]**

**India CCTV-AI for retail**
- **Wobot.ai** (Wobot Intelligence; Peak XV Surge alum): software layer on existing CCTV for operations and compliance across food, hospitality, retail, manufacturing and pharma.
  - Pricing: free tier up to 10 cameras, custom plans **from $40/location/month** (help article dated Mar 2022, likely stale). — [SafetyTechReview](https://safetytechreview.com/vendors/wobot) **[3P citing old official]**
  - Funding about $5.5M total — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/wobotai); about $2.5M per [Craft](https://craft.co/wobot-intelligence) (conflicting).
  - Customers: IRCTC, Rebel Foods, CureFit, Max, Kitopi, Smartworks — [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/organization/wobot-intelligence); Goodwill, Ocean State Job Lot, Mezeh — [YesPress](https://yespress.io/wobot-ai).
  - Has published retail conversion content. — [Wobot blog](https://wobot.ai/blog/part-1-maximizing-customer-conversions-in-retail-with-wobot-ai)
  - Critical take: "big logos, a thin revenue line". — [The Invincible India](https://theinvincibleindia.in/wobot-ai-deep-dive/)

Claims 100k+ cameras and 99.9% accuracy [Vendor claim]. No pricing found. — [Staqu](https://www.staqu.com/how-the-retail-industry-is-using-video-analytics-to-extract-data-from-cctv-streams/); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/the-cctv-gold-rush-indian-startups-oems-race-to-fill-surveillance-vacuum/)
- **Agrex.ai (AIVIS)**, New Delhi/Amsterdam: runs on existing Hikvision/Dahua/Axis/CP Plus cameras via an edge box. Covers shrinkage (sweethearting, fitting-room, tag removal), queues, footfall and gender, with POS integrations (GoFrugal, Marg, LS Central, Petpooja). - **IndoAI**: Starter **₹8,000/store/month** (up to 2 cameras), Growth **₹14,000/store/month** (up to 6 cameras), chain tier custom, multi-store discounts from 3+ stores. - The SaySo video module has to fit roughly ₹8–14K per store per month (Indian vendor anchors) or about $40/location/month (Wobot). It cannot charge US enterprise rates. The differentiator must be retail-sales-specific events (walkouts, unattended customers, wait-to-greet), not generic footfall or compliance.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Staqu, Tentosoft or Wobot's current plans.
- No Indian vendor found that explicitly sells "unattended customer" or "staff-to-customer attention" metrics; only queue, footfall and dwell were found.

## Q5. Evidence of demand and gaps (what nobody does)

### Takeaway
There is strong demand evidence in the US (Rilla's ARR and funding, dealership adoption, claimed 17–40% lifts) and early retail-floor adoption (Vera's logos). No competitor found combines wearable audio with CCTV video in one product; Borentis only claims to be "video-ready". Hinglish in-store audio has one known competitor (Borentis).

### Cited Findings
- Rilla's ARR of about $40M (2024) to about $70M (2026 est.) — [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/rilla); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/rilla). Claimed 40% close-rate lift and 17% ticket uplift — [Komo](https://komo.ai/directory/rilla) [Vendor claim].
- Agrex claims a 32% conversion uplift at Bata India — [Agrex](https://www.agrexai.com/what-is-retail-video-analytics/) [Vendor claim, no methodology].
- In-store audio is harder than call-centre audio: noisy ambient sound, multiple speakers, offline conditions and code-switched Indian languages. — [Borentis](https://borentis.com/learn/best-ai-in-store-sales-coaching-tools-india/)
- Phone-first Indian CI tools (Convin, Salesken, Enthu, Gong/Observe) do not support offline floor capture, according to Borentis's comparison. — [Borentis](https://borentis.com/learn/best-ai-in-store-sales-coaching-tools-india/) [Competitor's own claim]
- Vera explicitly needs "no cameras", i.e. it is audio-only. — [Vera AI](https://www.heyvera.ai/)
- Privacy and consent for recording customers came up as an unaddressed issue in search coverage. — [Retail Tech Innovation Hub](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/how-ai-wearables-are-transforming-retail-operations-customer-experience-and-store-productivity) (absence noted)

### Inferences
- **Gap 1, audio plus video fusion:** audio CI vendors (Rilla, Siro, Vera, tolq, Borentis) and CCTV vendors (Wobot, Staqu, Agrex, IndoAI) are separate. Linking "customer walked out" (video) to "what the salesperson said" (audio) appears unaddressed.
- **Gap 2, India retail floor:** only Borentis targets it, with no disclosed customers or pricing. The space is open but not empty.
- **Gap 3, price point:** global in-person CI at $150–350/rep is unaffordable for mass Indian retail. A per-store bundle in the ₹10–25K/month range (audio badges plus CCTV) would sit between Indian CCTV-AI pricing and global per-seat CI.
- India's DPDP Act consent requirements will apply to customer voice recording. Competitors' handling of this is not documented (needs legal research).

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) ROI studies found for in-store conversation recording.
- No Indian retailer case study for in-store audio AI found.
- Did not verify whether Wobot or Staqu offer staff-attention or walkout analytics specifically.
