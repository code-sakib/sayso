# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon: rules and logistics

Source: the host's words at the kickoff stream (Meta × Cerebral Valley), saved 29 Oct 2026.

## What this means for SaySo

| Rule | What we do |
|---|---|
| New work only, built during the hackathon | Every commit happens in this repo from now on. Research and design files were made on 27–28 Oct, after kickoff |
| Prior work must be called out | We have none. Say so in the submission form |
| Public GitHub repo, open source | Make the repo public before submitting. Add an open-source licence (MIT) |
| Demo only what was built during the event | The demo shows the badge, the pipeline and the coaching report we build this week |
| No legal, ethical or policy violations | Staff-only mode, a visible recording light, and only mock conversations with people who agreed to be recorded |
| Avoid basic ideas (chat wrappers, simple RAG, image analysers, medical advice) | SaySo is hardware + audio + video + reasoning, not a chat wrapper |
| Team size: 1–4, all approved | Solo, Indie track |
| Theme | **Build for Your World**: a problem you know firsthand, in your own domain |
| **3-minute demo video** with a good voiceover | The most important part of the submission. Plan the script and record it early |
| Deadline | **4 Nov 2026, 9 PM ET** (6:30 AM IST on 5 Nov). Review the submission form early |

## Judging

| Stage | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pass / fail | 5–8 Nov | Does it fit the theme and use Meta AI products? |
| 2. Scoring 0–10 | 5–8 Nov | Meta judges score on the rubric |
| 3. Live finals | 9 Nov | Top 5 per track. Judges watch the 3-minute video, give feedback, rank 1st–5th |

Rubric: **Impact** (real-world potential, who benefits, fit with the problem statement) · **Use of Meta AI** · **Technical depth** · **Demo**.
