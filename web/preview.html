How it works

Three steps from
walkout to better sales.

No new cameras. No dashboards to watch. One short note per salesperson, every day.

01 /

Badge listens

The salesperson wears SaySo. A red light shows when it records. Customer voices are removed automatically.

02 /

Camera sees

Your existing CCTV shows when a customer waited too long or walked out. Meta SAM 3.1 finds the moment.

03 /

SaySo coaches

Muse Spark links what was said to what happened, and writes what to say next time.
